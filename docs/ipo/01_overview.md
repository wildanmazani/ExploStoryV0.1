# Game Overview

## Summary

**Explo-Story** is a 2D top-down pixel-art adventure RPG for learning History (Sejarah KSSM, Form 4). The player is **Alif**, a Form 4 student who falls asleep the night before a History test and wakes up inside the Explo-Story world, where the villain **Nirbana** is spreading the *Kabus Lupa* (Fog of Forgetting) to make everyone forget their history.

Each chapter of the syllabus becomes a region of the world. Each subtopic becomes a mission (*Misi*) with its own place, guide character, puzzles and reward artifact. Finishing a mission clears the fog from that place and brings its colour back.

## Core loop

1. **Explore** the open world on a tile grid. Talk to villagers, read signs and follow the gold arrow to the active mission.
2. **Enter a mission area** (greyed out by Kabus Lupa) and meet its guide character.
3. **Solve the mission's puzzles**, which teach the subtopic through matching, sorting, investigation boards and objective questions. There is no timer and no penalty.
4. **Earn the reward artifact**, EXP and coins. The area regains its colour and a page is added to *Nota Alif*.
5. **Clear the chapter test** (*Ujian Bab*) by defeating the chapter boss, which opens the next chapter.
6. **Revise and compete** in the Bilik Pertarungan / Gelanggang Pesta Silat, where correct answers power up attacks.

## Game flow

Title → Login / Register / Guest → Prologue (watch-only, skippable) → Alif's bedroom (wake-up and tutorial) → Kampung Jejak → Misi 1.1 to 1.4 → Ujian Bab 1 → Pintu Kabus → Lembah Kebangkitan → Misi 2.1 to 2.8 → Ujian Bab 2 → Ending.
