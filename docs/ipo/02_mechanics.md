# Game Mechanics

## World structure: Arka, Zon and Misi

The syllabus is mapped onto three levels, shown on the **Arka & Zon** screen:

| Level | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arka | School form (Tingkatan) | Sejarah Tingkatan 4 |
| Zon | Chapter (Bab) | Bab 2: Kebangkitan Nasionalisme |
| Misi | Subtopic, played as one mission area | Misi 2.7: Maktab Perguruan Sultan Idris |

Locked chapters are shown with their titles so players can see the whole path ahead.

## Exploration

- **Grid movement:** the player walks one tile at a time (WASD / arrow keys, Shift to run) or taps a destination. A pathfinder routes Alif there.
- **Pau the companion** follows Alif and gives hints on request (H key or tapping Pau). Hints are logged for teachers.
- **Guidance:** an exclamation mark (!) marks the next task. A gold arrow on the minimap, the full map and the screen edge points to the active mission.
- **Kabus Lupa:** mission areas start grey under a thin white fog and regain colour once their mission is complete. The minimap and full map show the same fog.
- **Living world:** villagers wander. Chickens, ducks, fish, butterflies and birds animate the world, along with kitchen smoke and lamps.
- **Maps:** a minimap with three zoom levels, plus a full map with a legend of places, keywords and symbols. Keywords can be searched and tapped to show their location.
- **Jalan Pintas (fast travel):** jump straight to any unlocked place from the bag or the full map.
- **Area title cards** introduce each place on first visit and explain why Alif has come.

## Mission puzzles

Each mission is a sequence of steps and puzzles defined in data. Teachers can edit questions, answers and text without touching code.

| Puzzle type | How it plays |
| --- | --- |
| Seret dan Padankan (drag & match) | Drag items onto their matching targets, e.g. kingdoms onto their period |
| Papan Siasatan (investigation board) | Pin evidence cards to the correct question on a cork board |
| Objektif | Multiple-choice questions with an explanation after each answer |
| Suaikan | Pair related items from two columns |
| World puzzles | Act on the map itself, e.g. putting the five kingdom gates in order (Funan, Champa, Srivijaya, Melaka, Johor-Riau) |

There is no timer and no failure penalty. A wrong answer gives a gentle explanation and the player tries again.

## Rewards and progression

- **EXP and levels**, shown on the HUD with coins.
- **Artifacts:** each mission rewards a historical artifact, stored in **Beg Alif**. Tapping an artifact plays its story.
- **Kad Tokoh:** character cards for the historical figures met.
- **Nota Alif:** an illustrated notebook. One page unlocks per mission and summarises the subtopic.
- **Peta Jejak:** a stone monument that tracks chapter progress with flags.
- **Replay:** finished missions can be replayed for practice. Replays give no further rewards.

## Ujian Bab (chapter test)

At the end of each chapter the player faces a boss: **Panglima Derhaka** (Bab 1) or **Bayang Nirbana** (Bab 2).

- Questions are drawn at random from the chapter's question bank (12 per test).
- Alif has **3 lives**. **7 correct answers** defeat the boss. There is no time limit.
- The test must be passed to open the next chapter. On failure the player can retry or revise, and the boss waits on the map.

## Bilik Pertarungan and Gelanggang Pesta Silat

A revision arena where answering correctly powers up attacks. The question bank can be set to Bab 1, Bab 2 or Campur (mixed).

**Quiz battle:** answer correctly to attack a chosen opponent. The last one standing wins 20 coins. Bot opponents are four classmates: Aisyah, Ravi, Mei Ling and Daniel.

**Gelanggang Pesta Silat** is a side-view action brawl set in the school hall. Modes are 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2, 1 vs 2 and 1 vs 3, on Easy, Normal or Hard. Each round has three phases:

1. **SOALAN (15 s):** everyone answers the same question.
2. **KEPUTUSAN:** a correct answer gives *Semangat* (full-strength attacks) and drops a school item into the ring. The fastest correct answer earns *Jurus Kilat* (a special move). A wrong answer gives *Lemah* (30% damage).
3. **AKSI (10 s):** free fighting with punches, kicks, the J-J-K combo, flying kicks, blocking, and picking up and throwing items.

Effects are cartoon-only (stars, "PAK!", plasters, dizzy "PENGSAN"), with no blood.

## Arena Ulang Kaji

A training yard on each island (Kampung Jejak, and beside the padang in Lembah Kebangkitan). It has a lobby, other players sparring in pairs, training dummies and a **Papan Markah** leaderboard (Overall / State / Friends, ranked by total EXP). Guru Jalal offers the side quest *Jurus Ingatan* after the first fragment is recovered.

## Accessibility and settings

- **Mod Mudah Baca (easy-read mode):** a choice of Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic fonts, adjustable text size and text speed, and read-aloud voice.
- **Momen Tenang:** a calm-down overlay opened by holding Pau.
- Separate Music, Sound and Voice volumes. Menus in Bahasa Melayu or English.
- Interactive music that changes with the area and the situation.
