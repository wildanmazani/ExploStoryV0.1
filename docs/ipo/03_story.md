# Story and Characters

## Premise

It is 11:47 pm, the night before a History test. Alif's class group chat is busy while he sits in his room, half-studying. As he reads, keywords drift in and out of "Ingatan Alif" (Alif's memory) and the letters scatter. He falls asleep.

In his dream the port is in chaos: classmates no longer recognise each other inside the **Kabus Lupa**. In the **Gua Warisan** (Cave of Heritage), **Nirbana**, the hooded Penghulu of the Kabus Lupa, shatters the **Inskripsi Telaga Batu** into four fragments (*Serpihan*). He wants everyone to forget their history and give up.

Alif wakes in his bedroom inside the Explo-Story world. His companion **Pau** explains the artifact, the four fragments and the mission. Alif picks up his school bag, and Misi 1.1 begins.

## Bab 1: Warisan Negara Bangsa

Alif travels across the first island, from Kampung Jejak to the four mission areas. Each mission recovers one fragment of the inscription, and each fragment restores colour to its area. Nirbana taunts Alif along the way. When all four fragments are back, Alif faces **Panglima Derhaka** in Ujian Bab 1. The chapter closes with the first flag raised on the Peta Jejak, a new page in Nota Alif, and a teaser for Bab 2.

## Bab 2: Kebangkitan Nasionalisme

After Bab 1, the **Pintu Kabus** (Fog Gate) west of Kampung Jejak opens onto a second island: **Lembah Kebangkitan** (Valley of Awakening). It is an orderly 1920s colonial town with a Jalan Besar, a padang with a wakaf and a clock tower, rows of shophouses, and a steamship at the Dermaga.

The setting runs from the 1900s to 1941. The Kabus Lupa greys out every area until its mission is done. The eight missions follow one route, with a numbered sign in front of each place. Nirbana mocks Alif every two missions. In Misi 2.8, **Gabenor Sekatan** guards the Menara Pentadbiran. The chapter ends with Ujian Bab 2 against **Bayang Nirbana**.

Story interiors include the **Jelutong Press** printing house and the **Maktab SITC** (Sultan Idris Training College).
